The 110 Barbershop Experience

Welcome to 110 Barbershop, where the timeless art of traditional barbering meets modern precision and style. Located in the heart of Melville, NY, we've established ourselves as the premier destination for discerning gentlemen who demand excellence in their grooming experience.

Our master barbers are artists who combine years of expertise, meticulous attention to detail, and genuine passion for the craft. Every visit is more than just a haircut—it's a transformative experience that leaves you looking sharp, feeling confident, and ready to conquer the world. We take pride in creating a welcoming atmosphere where tradition, quality, and exceptional service come together.
